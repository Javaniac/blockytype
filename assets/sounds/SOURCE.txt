Sounds of the sound settings (play sound on click / on error / time warning).

Origin: Monkeytype, https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype
  frontend/static/sounds/  at commit 78a22fdbb94d3a48b879e49e38e6c2d80cd5a60f
  click1..7, click14..26 (sample sets), error1..4, timeWarning.wav

Changes: converted from WAV to Ogg Vorbis (mono, 44.1 kHz, quality 2) because Minecraft only plays Ogg Vorbis.
The click sets 8..13 (sine, sawtooth, square, triangle, pentatonic, wholetone) are generated in Monkeytype's
code (sound-controller.ts); here they are short synthesized notes (click8..11: 14 notes of a C major scale from
C4, 0.3 s; click12/13: two octaves of the scale from C5, 0.8 s) made with ffmpeg's aevalsrc.

License: the Monkeytype repository is GPL-3.0. It has no separate license or credits file for the audio files,
so their origin and license are not known beyond that. Replace or remove them if that is a problem for you.
index.txt lists the files and is read by the server to build the resource pack's sounds.json.
